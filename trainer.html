How to play

  1. 1D: read the trend. Draw support and resistance on 1D and 4H, then trade on 4H.
  2. S/R line: click an old swing high or low. Trend line: two clicks. Lines stay on every timeframe.
  3. Next bar ▸ until price reaches your line.
  4. Buy or Sell, drag the red STOP and green TARGET lines, then ▶ Start trade.

The range is drawn for you: the shaded bands are the top and bottom 30% of the last 50 candles. Price inside a band = a trade zone. Price in the unshaded middle = press Next bar and wait. The chip at the top says which one you are in.

Zoom: drag the price numbers on the right, drag the chart to move it, mouse wheel for time. Fill = next candle's open, 0.05% fee per side.
