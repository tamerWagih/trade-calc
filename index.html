One trade, three charts

The big trend says which way. An old unbroken box on the 4H says where. A 15m close says when.

Rules before every trade

Tick each one only when it is true right now. The trade is allowed only at 12 of 12. The list clears itself when you log the result.

Direction (daily)

Level (4H)

Entry (15m)

Size and hands

STEP 1 Daily

Decide the direction first

Daily chart, SMA 50 on it. Nothing else is looked at until this is answered.

Above a rising line: longs at blue boxes only
Below a falling line: shorts at red boxes only
Crossing or flat: no trade on this coin today
STEP 2 4H

Find an old, unbroken box

TSR on the 4H. A blue box is a swing low no candle body has closed below since. Box top is the candle body, box bottom is the wick. It shows up about 20 candles (3 days) after the low. That is fine: you want price to come back to it, not to catch the low.

STEP 3 4H1H

Wait for price to come back into the box

This takes days. Set one alert at the box top and close the app. When it fires, re-check step 1, then check the 1H: has a body closed below the box bottom? If yes, the level is dead.

STEP 4 15m

The confirmation candle, then enter

On the 15m the box is a wide band. Price pokes in, wicks under it, maybe more than once. You wait for one candle: it was inside or below the box and its body closes above the box top. Enter at that close.

Entry = close of the confirmation candle
Stop = a little under the lowest wick of the visit
Target = next 4H red box, at least 2R away or skip
STEP 5

Size it, set it, leave it

Risk 1% of the account. The Trade tab does the maths: type the entry as price, the stop, and 2:1 or better. One entry, one stop, one target, isolated 5x. Then hands off until the trade ends at one of them. Log WIN or LOSS, which also clears the rules list for the next trade.

Reference: with a 1R stop and 2R targets, after fees you need about 36 winners in 100 to break even. Random entries in a daily uptrend land near that. The method has edge only if the Journal tab shows clearly more after 40 trades.

MIRROR 15m

The same trade as a short

Daily below a falling SMA 50, so only red boxes above price count. Price rallies into the red box, wicks poke above it, then one 15m body closes below the box bottom. Enter there. Stop a little above the highest wick. Target the next 4H blue box, at least 2R.

THE TRADE YOU SKIP 4H

A bounce against the trend is not a trade

Daily trend down, price drops into an old blue box and bounces hard. The 15m will confirm. You still do not buy: dip-buying against the trend only paid on crash days and lost overall in every version tested. In a downtrend a support bounce is where you wait for the next red box to short.

WICKS VS CLOSES 15m1H

What counts as broken

TSR keeps the box on screen even with price under it for the last three candles, and its alert fires on a one-tick wick. You decide by candle bodies, never by the indicator.
